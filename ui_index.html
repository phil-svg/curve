        Borrowing User 
        

          Picking a market abstracts its whole book into one max-LTV
          borrower. Debt: LLV2 → the market's
          borrow_cap (the worst case the market permits); LLV1 has no
          cap → the actual on-chain total_debt at snapshot time;
          dust/empty markets fall back to $500k. Collateral: the
          minimum that can legally carry that debt — the market's max
          LTV is probed from the engine (/debtcap with the market's A,
          loan_discount, 4 bands), collateral = debt / maxLTV, then the debt
          is clamped against the exact cap re-probed at that size (band
          rounding is not perfectly scale-free). The borrower therefore sits
          exactly at max LTV. Both fields stay editable after a pick.
        

        
        
On = llamma-simulator anchoring: soft-liq starts a hair
        below the start spot for ANY A (zero cushion, worst-case landing; kills
        the grid-phase lottery). Off = on-chain max-LTV placement.

        What the funded principal is still worth to the lenders:
        lender equity = debt₀ − badDebt(t), where debt₀ is the
        borrower's debt at t=0 and badDebt = max(0, debt − x − p·y)
        — the slice of the loan the position no longer covers even at market
        prices. Repayments and hard liquidations return principal (no change here);
        only bad debt destroys it. Interest earned is not included.
